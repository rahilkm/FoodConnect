# 10 NGO To Needy Workflow

## Goal
Enable NGOs and volunteers to distribute food where need is active, even when communities are mobile or displaced.

## Key principle
Do not model the platform as requiring every beneficiary to have an account. Use hotspot intelligence and ground-network updates.

## Flow
1. NGO receives or accepts donation
2. NGO checks active hotspots and current time windows
3. NGO creates or updates route
4. NGO assigns volunteer
5. Volunteer picks up food
6. Volunteer reaches hotspot stops
7. If hotspot is inactive or displaced, volunteer flags issue
8. System suggests nearby active hotspots
9. NGO approves reroute if necessary
10. Delivery and impact are recorded

## Hotspot fields that matter operationally
- area name
- coordinates
- active time slots
- average people count
- category tags such as migrants, elderly cluster, station crowd
- displacement risk
- last verified time
- managing NGO
- notes from field team

## Distribution modes
- direct route to hotspot
- multi-stop route
- nearest active hotspot assignment
- volunteer route assignment from accepted donation

## Dignity requirements
- avoid storing direct public-facing identities of vulnerable individuals
- record aggregate impact, not exploitative beneficiary detail
- allow private notes for NGO operations
- emphasize fair and discreet distribution
